Best AI roles for DevOps, cloud and SRE engineers

Running AI in production is an infrastructure problem: GPUs, model serving, autoscaling, cost, monitoring and reliability. DevOps, cloud and SRE engineers already have most of these skills, which makes MLOps / AI platform engineering one of the most direct switches into AI.

Quick answer

For most DevOps, cloud and SRE engineers, the best AI role is MLOps / AI Platform Engineer (96% fit for a typical profile), followed by AI Forward Deployed Engineer (50%) and ML Engineer (40%). Why it usually fits: your DevOps / operations background transfers almost directly; you already have cloud and deployment skills; you're interested in AI infrastructure. A typical person makes the switch in about 3 months at 8 hours of study a week.

Recommended AI roles for DevOps, cloud and SRE engineers

Ranked by our scoring engine for a typical profile: 7–12 years of experience · works in DevOps / Cloud / SRE · codes in Python, Go / Rust · Python: comfortable · AI experience: used AI tools · enjoys "making systems reliable at scale" · can study 5–10 hours a week. Your own answers may change the order, so take the free consultation for a personal result.

  1. MLOps / AI Platform Engineer 96% fit

    Run AI reliably and cheaply at scale. Best match. About 3 months at 8 h/week.

    • Your DevOps / operations background transfers almost directly
    • You already have cloud and deployment skills
    • You're interested in AI infrastructure

  2. AI Forward Deployed Engineer 50% fit

    Deploy AI inside real businesses, hands-on with customers. Strong alternative. About 3.2 months at 8 h/week.

    • Your years of experience build client trust
    • You framed the business problem before reaching for tech
    • You can write the code to deliver solutions

  3. ML Engineer 40% fit

    Build, train and ship ML models into real products. Strong alternative. About 5.5 months at 8 h/week.

    • Your Python is at a working level
    • You spotted the accuracy trap — good ML intuition

Skills you already have that transfer to AI

  • Cloud, CI/CD and reliability — exactly what AI platforms need
  • 9+ years of experience — judgement, communication and ownership transfer to any AI role
  • Working Python — the main language of AI
  • Cloud and deployment skills — rare among AI learners
  • Strong reasoning — you got 4 of 4 check questions right

Advice for DevOps, cloud and SRE engineers moving into AI

  • Learn the ML basics (training vs inference, model artifacts, evaluation) so you can talk to ML teams in their language.
  • Serve an open model with vLLM on Kubernetes and add dashboards for latency and cost. That one project covers most interview topics.
  • Cost optimisation stories (GPU utilisation, caching, batching) are gold in interviews.

Your first steps towards MLOps / AI Platform Engineer

  1. ML Concepts for Infra Engineers (2-3 weeks): How training, inference, model artifacts and evaluation work — enough to talk to ML teams.
  2. Python for Automation (quick revision): Python scripting, packaging and testing at a level to work in ML codebases.
