What is Training serving skew?Training serving skew kya hai?

Training serving skew: When features are computed differently in training and in production, quietly hurting the model.Jab training aur production mein features alag tarah se bante hain, aur model chupke se kharab hota hai.

🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

Like practising cricket with a tennis ball and then playing the match with a leather ball.Tennis ball se cricket practice karna aur phir match leather ball se khelna.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

For example, a 30 day average in training versus a 7 day average in the live service. Prevent it with one shared feature pipeline or a feature store, logging live features and comparing them with training data.Jaise training mein 30 din ka average aur live service mein 7 din ka. Ek shared feature pipeline ya feature store, live features log karke training data se compare karne se ise roko.
