What is Chatbot?Chatbot kya hai?

Also called: Conversational AI

Chatbot: A program you talk with in natural language; modern chatbots are usually powered by LLMs.Program jisse aap normal bhasha mein baat karte ho; aaj ke chatbots aksar LLMs se chalte hain.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

Old chatbots followed scripted rules and buttons. LLM chatbots understand free text and generate answers, often grounded in company documents with RAG and connected to tools for actions. Good chatbots show sources, say when they don't know, and hand over to a human when needed.Purane chatbots scripted rules aur buttons follow karte the. LLM chatbots khula text samajhte aur jawaab banate hain, aksar RAG se company documents pe based aur actions ke liye tools se jude. Acche chatbots sources dikhate hain, na pata ho to batate hain, aur zaroorat ho to insaan ko saunp dete hain.

💡 Real exampleAsli example

A bank's assistant that answers card questions from policy documents.Bank ka assistant jo policy documents se card ke sawaalon ka jawaab deta hai.

🔗 Related termsJude hue shabd

  • LLMA very large neural network trained on huge amounts of text to predict the next word, which lets it write, answer and reason.Bahut bada neural network jo bahut saare text pe agla shabd predict karne ke liye train hua hai, jisse woh likh, jawaab de aur soch sakta hai.
  • RAGLetting an LLM answer from your documents: search for relevant passages first, then generate an answer from them with citations.LLM ko aapke documents se jawaab dene dena: pehle relevant hisse dhoondho, phir unhi se citations ke saath jawaab banao.
  • AgentAn LLM that works in a loop: plans a step, uses a tool, looks at the result, and decides the next step until the goal is done.Ek LLM jo loop mein kaam karta hai: step plan karta hai, tool use karta hai, result dekhta hai, aur goal poora hone tak agla step tay karta hai.
