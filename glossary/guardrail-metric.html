What is Guardrail metric?Guardrail metric kya hai?

Guardrail metric: A metric that must not get worse while you improve the main metric.Aisa metric jo main metric sudhaarte waqt bigadna nahi chahiye.

🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

Like dieting to lose weight while making sure your energy levels don't crash: one number improves, another must not get worse.Jaise weight kam karne ke liye diet karte hue dhyaan rakhna ki energy na gire: ek number sudhre, doosra kharaab na ho.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

In an A/B test or launch, the main metric might be conversion or time saved, while guardrails watch complaints, errors, latency, cost and fairness. A 'win' that breaks a guardrail is not a win.A/B test ya launch mein main metric conversion ya bacha time ho sakta hai, jabki guardrails complaints, errors, latency, cost aur fairness dekhte hain. Jo 'jeet' guardrail tode woh jeet nahi.

💡 Real exampleAsli example

AI replies cut handling time by 20%, but customer complaints must not rise.AI replies handling time 20% kam karte hain, par customer complaints nahi badhni chahiye.
