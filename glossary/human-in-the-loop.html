What is Human in the loop?Human in the loop kya hai?

Also called: HITL

Human in the loop: Keeping a person involved to review or approve AI output before it takes effect.AI output laagu hone se pehle use review ya approve karne ke liye insaan ko shaamil rakhna.

🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

Like a junior writing a letter and the manager reading and signing it before it is sent.Jaise junior letter likhta hai aur bhejne se pehle manager padh kar sign karta hai.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

For risky or regulated decisions, AI drafts or suggests and a human approves, edits or rejects. Human feedback also becomes training and evaluation data. As quality is proven, the share of cases needing review can be reduced.Risky ya regulated faislon mein AI draft ya suggest karta hai aur insaan approve, edit ya reject karta hai. Insaani feedback training aur evaluation data bhi banta hai. Quality sabit hone pe review wale cases ka hissa kam kiya ja sakta hai.

💡 Real exampleAsli example

A doctor signs every AI drafted discharge summary.Doctor har AI drafted discharge summary sign karta hai.

Questions people ask

What is Human in the loop?

Keeping a person involved to review or approve AI output before it takes effect. For risky or regulated decisions, AI drafts or suggests and a human approves, edits or rejects. Human feedback also becomes training and evaluation data. As quality is proven, the share of cases needing review can be reduced.
