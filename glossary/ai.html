📘 ExplainedVistaar se

AI is the big umbrella. Older AI used hand written rules; most modern AI is machine learning, where the system learns patterns from examples. Deep learning and generative AI (like ChatGPT or Claude) are parts of machine learning. In jobs, 'AI' usually means building products and systems around these models.AI ek bada umbrella hai. Purana AI haath se likhe rules use karta tha; aaj ka zyaadatar AI machine learning hai, jahan system examples se patterns seekhta hai. Questions people ask

What is AI?

Computer systems that do things we'd normally call smart: understand language, recognise images, make decisions. AI is the big umbrella.
