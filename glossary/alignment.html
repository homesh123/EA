AI dictionary · Evaluation and safety

What is Alignment?Alignment kya hai?

Alignment: Making AI models behave the way people intend: helpful, honest and safe.AI models ko waisa behave karwana jaisa log chahte hain: madadgaar, imaandaar aur safe.

🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

Like training a very capable new employee in the company's values, so they are helpful to customers, honest, and refuse harmful requests.Jaise ek bahut kaabil naye employee ko company ki values sikhaana, taaki woh customers ki madad kare, sach bole, aur nuksaan wali requests mana kar de.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

A model trained only to predict text can produce harmful, false or unhelpful output. Alignment techniques, such as instruction tuning, RLHF, DPO and constitutional methods, teach the model to follow instructions, refuse harmful requests and admit uncertainty. In products, alignment is supported by system prompts, guardrails and evaluation.Sirf text predict karne ke liye train hua model nuksaandeh, galat ya bekaar output de sakta hai. Alignment techniques, jaise instruction tuning, RLHF, DPO aur constitutional methods, model ko instructions maanna, nuksaandeh requests mana karna aur anishchitata maanna sikhaati hain. Products mein alignment ko system prompts, guardrails aur evaluation sahaara dete hain.

💡 Real exampleAsli example

A chatbot politely refusing to explain how to make a weapon is alignment at work.Chatbot ka hathiyar banane ka tarika batane se vinamrata se mana karna alignment ka kaam hai.
