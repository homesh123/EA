What is Decision tree?Decision tree kya hai?

Decision tree: A model that predicts by asking a series of yes or no questions about the input.Model jo input ke baare mein haan ya nahi sawaalon ki ek series poochh kar predict karta hai.

🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

Like the game '20 questions': each yes or no question narrows things down until you reach the answer.'20 questions' game jaisa: har haan ya na wala sawaal options kam karta hai jab tak jawaab na mil jaaye.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

Each split picks the question that best separates the outcomes, like 'income above ₹50k?'. Trees are easy to explain but a single deep tree overfits. Random forests and gradient boosting combine many trees and are among the strongest models for tabular data.Har split woh sawaal chunta hai jo results ko sabse acche se alag kare, jaise 'income ₹50k se zyaada?'. Trees samjhaane mein aasaan hain par ek gehra tree overfit karta hai. Random forests aur gradient boosting kai trees jodte hain aur tabular data ke liye sabse mazboot models mein hain.

💡 Real exampleAsli example

Questions people ask

What is Decision tree?

A model that predicts by asking a series of yes or no questions about the input. Each split picks the question that best separates the outcomes, like 'income above ₹50k?'. Trees are easy to explain but a single deep tree overfits. Random forests and gradient boosting combine many trees and are among the strongest models for tabular data.

How can I understand Decision tree in simple words?

Like the game '20 questions': each yes or no question narrows things down until you reach the answer.

What is an example of Decision tree?

Has the customer missed a payment? If yes, high risk; if no, check income next.

Decision tree ka matlab kya hai?
