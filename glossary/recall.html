What is Recall?Recall kya hai?

Also called: Sensitivity, true positive rate

Recall: Of all the real positives, the share the model caught.Saare asli positives mein se model ne kitne pakde, uska hissa.

🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

Of all the real fires, how many did the alarm catch? High recall means few fires are missed.Saari asli aag mein se alarm ne kitni pakdi? High recall matlab kam aag chhooti.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

Recall = true positives ÷ (true positives + false negatives). High recall means few misses, which matters when missing a case is costly, like cancer screening or fraud. Lowering the threshold usually increases recall and lowers precision.Recall = true positives ÷ (true positives + false negatives). High recall matlab kam miss, jo tab zaroori hai jab case miss karna mehenga ho, jaise cancer screening ya fraud. Threshold ghataane se aam taur pe recall badhta aur precision ghatti hai.

💡 Real exampleAsli example

There were 10 frauds and the model caught 8: recall is 80%.10 fraud the aur model ne 8 pakde: recall 80%.
