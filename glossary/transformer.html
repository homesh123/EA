🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

Transformers turn tokens into embeddings, then stack layers of self attention and feed forward networks with residual connections and normalisation. They process all tokens in parallel, which made training on huge datasets practical. They also power vision, speech and multimodal models.Transformers tokens ko embeddings mein badalte hain, phir self attention aur feed forward networks ki layers residual connections aur normalisation ke saath stack karte hain. Yeh saare tokens parallel mein process karte hain, jisse bade datasets pe training practical hui. Vision, speech aur multimodal models bhi inhi se chalte hain.

Questions people ask

What is Transformer?

The neural network architecture behind modern LLMs, built around attention. Transformers turn tokens into embeddings, then stack layers of self attention and feed forward networks with residual connections and normalisation. They process all tokens in parallel, which made training on huge datasets practical. They also power vision, speech and multimodal models.

How can I understand Transformer in simple words?

Like a reader who looks at the whole sentence at once and links every word to the words that matter for it, instead of reading strictly one word at a time.
