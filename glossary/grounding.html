What is Grounding?Grounding kya hai?

Grounding: Making an LLM base its answer on provided sources or tool results, not just its memory.LLM ke jawaab ko diye gaye sources ya tool results pe based banana, sirf uski yaad pe nahi.

🧸 Think of it likeAise samjho

Like an open book exam: answer from the book in front of you, not from fuzzy memory.Open book exam jaisa: saamne rakhi kitaab se jawaab do, dhundhli yaad se nahi.

📘 ExplainedVistaar se

Grounded answers come from retrieved documents, databases or APIs placed in the prompt, ideally with citations. Grounding reduces hallucinations and keeps answers current. Instruct the model to answer only from the sources and to say when they don't contain the answer.Grounded jawaab prompt mein daale gaye retrieved documents, databases ya APIs se aate hain, behtar ho to citations ke saath. Grounding hallucinations kam karti hai aur jawaab current rakhti hai. Model ko kaho sirf sources se jawaab de aur bataaye jab sources mein jawaab na ho.

💡 Real exampleAsli example

Answering 'what is my leave balance?' by reading the HR system, not guessing.'Meri leave balance kitni hai?' ka jawaab andaaze se nahi, HR system padh kar dena.
