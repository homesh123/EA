💼 Landing the AI role: resume, applications and interviews

EnglishHinglish

🏠 Real-life example

Selling a house, you don't list 'has walls and a roof'. You lead with what buyers care about: 'sea-facing, 5 minutes from the metro, renovated kitchen'. A resume works the same way: lead with results the employer cares about, in their language, backed by proof.

💡 In simple words

Tailor your resume to the role's keywords, and write bullets as action + result + number ('Built a RAG assistant that cut policy look-up time by 70%'). Put AI projects near the top, with links. Reframe past experience in AI terms: test automation becomes AI evaluation, backend APIs become LLM integration. Apply to the specific titles from your roadmap, and try internal moves at your current company. Prepare for coding rounds, system design (ML or LLM system design for AI roles), and deep-dives into your projects. Apply consistently, track applications, and learn from each interview.

🪜 Step by step

  1. Pick one target role and tailor your resume to it with the exact skills from real job descriptions.
  2. Lead with AI projects and results if you're switching careers, and rewrite past work to show transferable skills.
  3. Apply through referrals where possible: message people in target teams with a specific, short note.
  4. Prepare for interviews in order: introduction, fundamentals, role skills, design, behavioural stories, questions to ask.
  5. Track applications in a sheet, follow up after a week, and learn from every rejection.

🔑 Key terms

Impact bullet
A resume line written as action + result + number.
System design round
An interview where you design a whole system on a whiteboard.
Internal mobility
Moving to an AI role inside your current company.

Same experience, rewritten in AI terms with measurable impact.

⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

  • Sending the same resume to every job. Tailored resumes pass ATS and impress recruiters far more often.
  • Waiting until you feel 100% ready. Start applying once you have two solid projects and keep learning.

✅ Quick check

  2. Responsible for AI tasks

Show answer

C. Built a churn model with XGBoost that improved retention-campaign ROI by 22%: It shows what you did, how, and the measurable result.

🙋 Questions learners ask

Should I switch internally first?

If your company has AI projects, an internal move is often the fastest path: you already have trust and domain knowledge.

Will I have to take a pay cut?

Not usually if you move to a role that uses your experience, like a backend engineer becoming an LLM engineer. Starting from zero in a new field is when cuts happen.

📌 Remember

Action + result + number. Reframe your experience in AI terms. Apply steadily.
