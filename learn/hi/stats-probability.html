🎲 Probability aur distributions

EnglishHinglish

🏠 Real-life example

Weather app bolta hai '70% chance of rain'. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki din ke 70% hisse mein baarish hogi. Matlab hai ki aise dinon mein 10 mein se 7 baar baarish hui thi. AI models bhi aise hi baat karte hain: '92% likely spam' ek probability hai, guarantee nahi.

💡 Aasaan shabdon mein

Probability 0 (impossible) se 1 (pakka) tak ka number hai jo batata hai ki kuch hone ke kitne chances hain. Distribution batati hai ki har possible value kitni likely hai. Normal distribution, yaani bell curve, bahut common hai: height, exam scores aur measurement errors average ke aas-paas jama hote hain, aur extremes pe kam ho jaate hain. Bahut saare ML models probability output karte hain, aur aap ek threshold chunte ho, jaise 0.5, unhe decision mein badalne ke liye. Conditional probability, P(A given B), A ka chance hai jab aapko pata ho ki B ho chuka hai.

🔑 Key terms

Probability
Kuch hone ka chance, 0 se 1 tak.
Normal distribution
Ghante (bell) jaisi curve jahan zyaadatar values average ke paas hoti hain.
Threshold
Cut-off jo probability ko yes/no decision mein badalta hai.

👀 Example dekho

text
Model says: P(fraud) = 0.83 for this UPI transaction
Threshold 0.5  → flag it  ✅
Threshold 0.9  → let it pass

Lower threshold = catch more fraud, but annoy more honest customers.

Threshold chunna sirf maths ka nahi, business ka decision bhi hai.

✅ Quick check

Model bolta hai email 'spam with probability 0.6' hai. Iska matlab kya hai?

  1. Email ka 60% hissa spam hai
  2. Spam hone ke chances zyaada hain, lekin pakka nahi
  3. Yeh pakka spam hai
Jawaab dekho

B. Spam hone ke chances zyaada hain, lekin pakka nahi: 0.6 ka matlab hai ki aisi bahut saari emails mein se lagbhag 10 mein se 6 spam hongi. Likely hai, pakka nahi.

📌 Yaad rakho

Model ka output aksar probability hota hai, aur threshold aap chunte ho.
