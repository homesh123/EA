🔗 Correlation vs causation, aur sampling bias

EnglishHinglish

🏠 Asli zindagi ki misaal

Garmiyon mein ice-cream ki sales aur doobne ke cases dono badhte hain. Dono correlated hain, lekin ice-cream doobne ki wajah nahi hai. Garmi dono ki wajah hai. Aur agar aap fitness habits ka survey sirf gym wale logon se karoge, to result poore shehar ko represent nahi karega. Yeh hai sampling bias.

💡 Aasaan shabdon mein

Correlation ka matlab hai do cheezein saath-saath badalti hain. Causation ka matlab hai ek cheez sach mein doosri ko cause karti hai. ML models correlations dhoondhne mein bahut ache hain, lekin khud causation prove nahi kar sakte, isliye 'feature X causes Y' jaise claims mein savdhaan raho. Sampling bias tab hota hai jab aapka data real duniya ko represent nahi karta, aur biased data pe train hua model biased predictions karega. Kisi bhi result pe bharosa karne se pehle poocho: is data mein kaun missing hai, aur is pattern ko aur kya explain kar sakta hai?

🪜 Step by step

  1. Correlation naapta hai do cheezein saath kaise chalti hain, −1 (ulta) se 0 (koi rishta nahi) se +1 (saath) tak.
  2. Teesra factor (confounder) dhoondo: chhatri ki bikri aur traffic jam dono baarish ki wajah se badhte hain.
  3. Dekho data mein kaun hai (sampling bias): app users ka survey un logon ke baare mein kuch nahi batata jinhone app hata diya.
  4. Cause prove karne ke liye randomised experiment (A/B test) karo: users ko randomly baanto taaki farak sirf aapke change ka ho.
  5. Jab experiment na ho sake, shabd dhyaan se chuno: 'causes' nahi, 'is associated with' bolo.

🔑 Zaroori shabd

Correlation
Do variables saath-saath badalte hain.
Causation
Ek cheez seedha doosri ko cause karti hai.
Sampling bias
Aisa data jo poori population ko represent nahi karta.

👀 Example dekho

text
Finding: users who use the app's dark mode spend more money.
❌ 'Dark mode makes people spend more' (causation claim)
✅ Maybe power users like dark mode AND spend more.
   Test it: an A/B experiment that randomly switches dark mode on.

Correlation se causation tak pahunchne ka tareeka randomised experiment hai.

⚠️ Aam galtiyaan jinse bachna hai

  • Yeh maan lena ki feature kaam karta hai kyunki uske users zyaada khush hain. Engaged users feature chunte hain, ulta nahi.
  • Biased samples pe model train karna, jaise sirf approved loans. Model reject hue logon ke baare mein kabhi nahi seekhta.

✅ Jaldi se check karo

Hiring model sirf ek city ke purane hires pe train hua hai. Risk kya hai?

  1. Woh slow hoga
  2. Koi risk nahi
  3. Sampling bias: doosri cities ke logon ko galat score de sakta hai
Jawaab dekho

C. Sampling bias: doosri cities ke logon ko galat score de sakta hai: Training data un sab logon ko represent nahi karta jinhe model judge karega.

🙋 Seekhne wale yeh poochhte hain

Kya ML models causes dhoondh sakte hain?

Normal ML prediction ke liye useful correlations dhoondhta hai, causes nahi. Causal sawaalon ke liye experiments ya special causal inference methods chahiye.

Kya 0.3 ka correlation weak hai?

Yeh thoda hai, par business mein phir bhi matter kar sakta hai. Aur correlation sirf seedhi line wale rishte naapta hai; hamesha data plot karo.

📌 Yaad rakho

Correlation causation nahi hai, aur biased data se biased AI banta hai.
