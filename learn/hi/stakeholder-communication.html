🗣️ Non-technical logon ko AI samjhaana

EnglishHinglish

🏠 Asli zindagi ki misaal

Achha CA dukaandaar ko tax law section by section nahi samjhaata. Woh kehta hai: 'Agar aap X karoge, to is saal ₹40,000 bachenge, aur ek risk yeh hai.' Business logon ko AI samjhaana bhi aisa hi hai: nateeje se shuru karo, unke shabd use karo, aur risks ke baare mein imaandaar raho.

💡 Aasaan shabdon mein

Forward deployed engineers, PMs aur consultants ka bahut time aise logon ke saath jaata hai jinhe embeddings se matlab nahi. Unhe time, paisa, risk aur apne customers se matlab hai. Updates aise structure karo: nateeja ya chahiye decision, saboot (demo ya numbers), risks aur unhe kaise sambhaal rahe ho, phir next steps. Jargon ki jagah analogies use karo. Working prototype jaldi dikhao, kyunki woh das slides se behtar hai. Discovery meetings mein bolne se zyaada suno, aur requirements likhit mein confirm karo. AI kya nahi kar sakta, yeh pehle hi saaf batao: bharosa aapki sabse keemti cheez hai.

🪜 Step by step

  1. Us nateeje se shuru karo jiski unhe parwah hai: bacha time, paisa, risk, customers.
  2. Kaise kaam karta hai ek rozmarra ki misaal se samjhao, technical shabdon se nahi.
  3. Limits ke baare mein imaandaar raho: kitni baar galat hota hai, aur usse kya bachata hai.
  4. Batao mat, dikhao: unke asli data pe chhota demo slides se zyaada convince karta hai.
  5. Ek saaf decision ya agle step ke saath khatam karo jo aapko unse chahiye.

🔑 Zaroori shabd

Stakeholder
Koi bhi jo project se affect hota ho ya uspe decide karta ho.
Discovery
Asli problem, users aur constraints samajhne ki meetings.
Executive summary
3–4 line ka version: nateeja, saboot, risk, maang.

👀 Example dekho

text
❌ "We implemented hybrid retrieval with a re-ranker and improved MRR@10 by 0.18."
✅ "The assistant now finds the right policy 9 times out of 10, up from 7.
    Agents save ~4 minutes per ticket (≈ ₹6 lakh/month).
    Risk: rare wrong answers → it always shows its source.
    Decision needed: roll out to 2 more teams?"

Kaam same hai, bas nateeja → saboot → risk → maang ki tarah samjhaaya gaya.

⚠️ Aam galtiyaan jinse bachna hai

  • Zyaada vaada karna ("yeh 100% accurate hoga"). Pehli galti pe hi bharosa toot jaata hai.
  • Logon ko aise metrics mein dubona jo woh samajh na sakein. Numbers ko impact mein translate karo.

✅ Jaldi se check karo

Business leader ko update dete waqt shuruaat kis se karni chahiye?

  1. Aapka tech stack
  2. Model architecture ki details
  3. Business nateeja aur aapko kaunsa decision chahiye
Jawaab dekho

C. Business nateeja aur aapko kaunsa decision chahiye: Leaders nateejon aur risks pe decide karte hain. Pehle woh do, details maangne pe.

🙋 Seekhne wale yeh poochhte hain

Business leader ko accuracy kaise samjhaun?

Ginti aur nateejon mein: "100 invoices mein se 95 sahi padhta hai; baaki 5 ek insaan ke check ke liye flag hote hain."

Agar stakeholder ko dar ho ki AI unki team ko replace kar dega?

Maano, dikhao tool kaise boring kaam hataata hai, team ko testing mein shaamil karo, aur batao roles kaise badlenge.

📌 Yaad rakho

Nateeja → saboot → risk → maang. Jargon se behtar analogies. Slides se behtar demos.
