💼 AI role paana: resume, applications aur interviews

EnglishHinglish

🏠 Asli zindagi ki misaal

Ghar bechte waqt aap 'deewarein aur chhat hai' nahi likhte. Aap woh likhte ho jo khareedne wale ko chahiye: 'sea-facing, metro se 5 minute, nayi kitchen'. Resume bhi aise hi kaam karta hai: employer ko jo nateeje chahiye unse shuru karo, unki bhasha mein, saboot ke saath.

💡 Aasaan shabdon mein

Resume ko role ke keywords ke hisaab se tailor karo, aur bullets action + nateeja + number ki tarah likho ('RAG assistant banaaya jisne policy dhoondhne ka time 70% kam kiya'). AI projects links ke saath upar rakho. Purane experience ko AI ki bhasha mein dikhao: test automation AI evaluation ban jaata hai, backend APIs LLM integration ban jaati hain. Apne roadmap ke specific titles pe apply karo, aur current company mein internal move bhi try karo. Coding rounds, system design (AI roles ke liye ML ya LLM system design) aur apne projects ke deep-dives ki taiyaari karo. Lagaataar apply karo, applications track karo, aur har interview se seekho.

🪜 Step by step

  1. Ek target role chuno aur asli job descriptions ke exact skills ke saath resume uske hisaab se banao.
  2. Career switch kar rahe ho to AI projects aur results pehle rakho, aur purane kaam ko transferable skills dikhaane ke liye dobara likho.
  3. Jahan ho sake referrals se apply karo: target teams ke logon ko ek specific, chhota note bhejo.
  4. Interviews ki taiyaari order mein karo: introduction, fundamentals, role skills, design, behavioural stories, poochne wale sawaal.
  5. Applications ek sheet mein track karo, ek hafte baad follow up karo, aur har rejection se seekho.

🔑 Zaroori shabd

Impact bullet
Resume ki line jo action + nateeja + number ki tarah likhi ho.
System design round
Interview jahan whiteboard pe poora system design karna hota hai.
Internal mobility
Apni current company ke andar AI role mein jaana.

Weekly rhythm: 10 tailored applications · 2 referral asks · 1 mock interview

Experience same hai, bas AI ki bhasha mein aur naape jaa sakne wale impact ke saath likha gaya.

⚠️ Aam galtiyaan jinse bachna hai

  • Har job ko same resume bhejna. Tailored resumes ATS aur recruiters dono ko kahin zyaada baar paas karte hain.
  • 100% ready mehsoos hone tak intezaar karna. Do mazboot projects hote hi apply karna shuru karo aur seekhte raho.

✅ Jaldi se check karo

Kaunsa resume bullet sabse strong hai?

  1. Machine learning se parichit
  2. AI tasks ki zimmedaari
  3. XGBoost se churn model banaaya jisne retention-campaign ROI 22% badhaaya
Jawaab dekho

C. XGBoost se churn model banaaya jisne retention-campaign ROI 22% badhaaya: Yeh dikhaata hai ki aapne kya kiya, kaise kiya, aur naapa ja sakne wala nateeja.

🙋 Seekhne wale yeh poochhte hain

Kya pehle company ke andar switch karun?

Agar company mein AI projects hain, to internal move aksar sabse tez raasta hai: bharosa aur domain knowledge pehle se hai.

Kya salary kam leni padegi?

Aam taur pe nahi, agar aap aise role mein jao jo aapka experience use kare, jaise backend engineer se LLM engineer. Naye field mein zero se shuru karne pe kami hoti hai.

📌 Yaad rakho

Action + nateeja + number. Apna experience AI ki bhasha mein likho. Lagaataar apply karo.
