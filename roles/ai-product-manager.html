What does an AI Product Manager do?

Best fit for product/project managers, business analysts, team leads and domain experts from non-IT fields. You don't need to code heavily: you need to understand what AI can and can't do, define the right problems, measure quality and lead teams to ship.

Core skills

  • AI literacy (LLMs, ML, limits)
  • Problem framing
  • AI evaluation & metrics
  • No-code prototyping
  • Stakeholder leadership

Who usually moves into this role

  • Non-IT professionals (finance, healthcare, teaching, operations)
  • Product managers, project managers and business analysts
  • Senior engineers, architects and tech leads (10+ years)

AI Product Manager roadmap: 6 steps

Follow the steps in order. Times assume about 10 study hours a week. Take the free questionnaire to get a personalised version that skips what you already know and fits your weekly hours.

  1. Step 1: AI Literacy for Decision-Makers 2-3 weeks

    How LLMs and ML models work, what they're good and bad at, costs, risks and hallucinations.

    Why it matters: You can't make good product decisions about technology you don't understand.

    Learn from: AI for Everyone — Andrew Ng (audit free), Anthropic & OpenAI docs overviews (free)

    Topics: LLMs vs classic ML, Tokens, context & cost, Hallucinations, Build vs buy

    Must-know: Explain RAG and fine-tuning to a non-technical person

  2. Step 2: Hands-On Prototyping (Low-Code) 2-3 weeks

    Build prototypes with prompting, no-code tools and simple scripts so you can test ideas yourself.

    Why it matters: PMs who can prototype get buy-in faster and earn engineers' respect.

    Learn from: Claude / ChatGPT projects, n8n or Zapier AI (free tiers), Streamlit (free)

    Topics: Prompt design, No-code workflows, Simple RAG with tools, Rapid user testing

    Must-know: 3 working prototypes

  3. Step 3: Data & Metrics Basics 2-3 weeks

    Basic SQL, dashboards, A/B testing and how to read model metrics.

    Why it matters: AI products live or die by measurement — you'll decide what 'good enough' means.

    Learn from: SQLBolt (free), Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments (book)

    Topics: SQL basics, A/B testing, Precision vs recall trade-offs, Business KPIs

    Must-know: Define success metrics for an AI feature

  4. Step 4: AI Product Discovery, Evaluation & Risk 3 weeks

    Find high-value AI use cases, write AI product specs, design eval sets, and manage risk, safety and compliance.

    Why it matters: This is the core craft of AI product management.

    Learn from: Lenny's Newsletter AI PM posts, Google People + AI Guidebook (free)

    Topics: Use-case prioritisation, AI PRDs, Eval rubrics, Responsible AI

    Must-know: An AI PRD with an eval plan

  5. Step 5: AI Product Case Studies 3-4 weeks

    Two case studies in your industry: problem, prototype, metrics, risks and rollout plan.

    Why it matters: Case studies replace 'years of AI PM experience' in interviews.

    Learn from: Your domain experience, public datasets

    Topics: Opportunity sizing, Prototype, Metrics, Go-to-market

    Must-know: Case study deck + prototype link

    Project ideas
    AI feature PRD + prototype: Spec, eval set and working prototype for an AI feature in your industry
    Workflow automation case study: Map a manual process, automate it with no-code AI tools, measure time saved

  6. Step 6: Target AI PM / Transformation Roles ongoing

    Apply for AI PM, AI Product Owner, AI Business Analyst roles — or lead an AI initiative where you work now.

    Why it matters: Internal AI initiatives are the most common path into AI PM.

    Learn from: LinkedIn, company career pages

    Topics: Product sense interviews, AI strategy questions

    Must-know: Lead with domain + AI impact
