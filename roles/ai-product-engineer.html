What does an AI Product Engineer do?

Best fit for web, mobile and full-stack developers. You keep your app-building strength and add LLM APIs, RAG and evaluation on top. This is one of the fastest switches because most of the job is still product engineering.

Core skills

  • LLM APIs
  • RAG & vector search
  • TypeScript / Python
  • Prompt design & evals
  • Product sense

Who usually moves into this role

  • Software testers and QA engineers
  • Java, .NET and backend developers
  • Frontend and full-stack developers
  • Senior engineers, architects and tech leads (10+ years)

AI Product Engineer roadmap: 6 steps

Follow the steps in order. Times assume about 10 study hours a week. Take the free questionnaire to get a personalised version that skips what you already know and fits your weekly hours.

  1. Step 1: LLM API Fundamentals 1-2 weeks

    Call Claude / GPT APIs, structure prompts, stream responses, handle tokens, costs and errors.

    Why it matters: This is the building block of every AI feature you will ship.

    Learn from: Anthropic API docs (free), OpenAI API docs (free), Vercel AI SDK docs (free)

    Topics: Messages & system prompts, Streaming, Structured (JSON) output, Tool / function calling, Cost & rate limits

    Must-know: Streaming chat UI; Structured output

  2. Step 2: Python Working Knowledge 2 weeks

    Enough Python to read AI examples, write small services and use AI SDKs.

    Why it matters: Most AI libraries and examples are Python-first, even if your product is in TypeScript.

    Learn from: CS50P (free), Python for JavaScript developers guides (free)

    Topics: Syntax for JS/Java developers, Virtual environments, FastAPI basics

    Must-know: Build one small FastAPI endpoint

  3. Step 3: RAG with a Vector Database 3-4 weeks

    Retrieval-Augmented Generation: chunk documents, embed them, retrieve the relevant pieces, and let the LLM answer from them.

    Why it matters: RAG is the most common AI feature companies ask for — 'chat with our documents'.

    Learn from: Pinecone Learn (free), LlamaIndex / LangChain RAG tutorials (free)

    Topics: Embeddings, Chunking, Hybrid search, Citations, pgvector / Pinecone

    Must-know: RAG app with source citations

  4. Step 4: Evaluation, Guardrails & UX for AI 2 weeks

    Measure answer quality, handle hallucinations, design UX for uncertainty, add safety checks.

    Why it matters: Demos are easy; reliable AI features are hard — this is what senior AI engineers are paid for.

    Learn from: Hamel Husain's evals posts (free), Anthropic prompt engineering guide (free)

    Topics: Eval datasets, LLM-as-judge, Guardrails, Streaming UX & fallbacks

    Must-know: An eval set of 30+ real questions

  5. Step 5: Ship 3 AI Feature Demos 4-5 weeks

    A chat-with-docs app, an AI summariser/extractor, and an agentic or copilot feature — deployed with live URLs.

    Why it matters: Live, polished demos are the fastest way to get interviews for this role.

    Learn from: Vercel / Netlify (free), Hugging Face Spaces (free)

    Topics: Polished UI, Deployment, Usage analytics, Write-ups

    Must-know: 3 live demo links

    Project ideas
    Chat with your docs: Next.js + RAG app with citations and an eval script
    Invoice / resume extractor: Structured data extraction from PDFs with an LLM

  6. Step 6: Target AI Product Roles ongoing

    Apply for AI Engineer, AI Product Engineer and Full-Stack (AI) roles; at your current company, volunteer for the AI feature.

    Why it matters: Internal moves are often the fastest switch — you already know the product.

    Learn from: LinkedIn, Wellfound, company career pages

    Topics: AI system design, Take-home AI projects

    Must-know: Explain your eval approach in interviews
