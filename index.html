Which AI role should I choose?

Choose the AI role that builds on the work you already do. Backend developers usually fit LLM / Agent Engineer. Frontend, full-stack and mobile developers fit AI Product Engineer. DevOps and cloud engineers fit MLOps / AI Platform Engineer. Data engineers and analysts fit Data Engineer (AI) or ML Engineer. Senior engineers who enjoy clients fit AI Forward Deployed Engineer. PMs, BAs and non-IT professionals fit AI Product Manager. Freshers should start with AI Foundations. People with strong maths can aim for AI Research Engineer. Your working style, maths comfort and weekly study time also matter, and the questionnaire above weighs all of them.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI career consultation?

An AI career consultation helps you decide which AI role suits you and how to get there. This free online version asks about your experience, skills, preferred kind of work and goals, then recommends your best-fit AI role out of 9, explains why, shows your skill gaps and gives you a dated learning roadmap.

I'm confused about which AI role to choose. How do I get clarity?

Start from what you already do, pick by the kind of work you enjoy day to day, be honest about your coding and maths comfort, and match the role to the hours you can study. Then test your top choice with a small project. The consultation above applies these steps for you, and our guide on how to choose the right AI role explains them in detail.

Is this AI career counselling really free?

Yes. It is completely free, needs no sign-up, and runs in your browser. Your answers are never sent to a server.

Which AI roles are in highest demand in 2026?

LLM / Agent Engineers, AI Product Engineers and AI Forward Deployed Engineers are among the most in demand, followed by MLOps / AI Platform, ML Engineers and AI Data Engineers. AI Research roles pay the most but are highly selective.

What is the best AI role for a software engineer?

It depends on what you already do and enjoy. Backend developers often fit LLM / Agent Engineer, frontend and full-stack developers fit AI Product Engineer, DevOps engineers fit MLOps / AI Platform, and data engineers fit Data Engineer (AI). Senior engineers who enjoy working with clients fit Forward Deployed Engineer. The questionnaire weighs all of this for you.

My skills feel outdated. Do I have to start from zero?

No. Most AI jobs are still engineering, data or product jobs with AI added. The roadmap marks the steps you already know as fast-track, so you only spend time on real gaps.

Can a fresher or MCA graduate start an AI career?

Yes. Freshers and MCA/BCA graduates usually start with the AI Foundations path: Python, statistics, SQL and machine learning, proven with portfolio projects. If you already have strong ML projects, we recommend a direct path instead.

I don't code. Is there an AI role for me?

Yes. AI Product Manager and AI Business Analyst roles value domain knowledge and problem framing more than coding. Accountants, teachers, healthcare and operations professionals bring industry knowledge that AI teams need.

How long does it take to become job-ready in AI?

Usually 3 to 9 months, depending on your starting point, target role and weekly study hours. Your result shows an estimate based on the hours you can actually commit.

What programming language is best for AI?

Python. Most ML and LLM libraries are Python-first. JavaScript/TypeScript is also widely used for AI product features.
